What's new in 1.7.1

Reminders on time after a pause
After a 30 or 60 minute pause, the first eye rest reminder now comes 20 minutes after the pause ends instead of about 40.

Popups stay above the taskbar
A popup set to Bottom Right or Bottom Center no longer sinks behind the taskbar on setups with monitors at different scaling.

Dragging between monitors
When you drag a popup to a monitor with different scaling, it now stays under your cursor instead of jumping away.
